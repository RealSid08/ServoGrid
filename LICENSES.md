# Licences and attributions

ServoGrid currently has no third-party package-manager dependencies. It uses Apple platform frameworks including SwiftUI, MapKit, CoreLocation, BackgroundTasks, UserNotifications, Foundation, Observation, and XCTest under the terms of the Apple developer toolchain and platform SDKs.

Fuel data remains subject to the originating provider's terms. The app preserves provider attribution and source links in normalized snapshots and the station UI. Current source decisions and authoritative references are listed in `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`.

The national demo JSON and ServoGrid identity assets are project-created evaluation material. They are not provider data and must not be represented as live fuel evidence.

No repository-wide open-source licence has been selected in this build; that is a project-owner decision before public redistribution.
